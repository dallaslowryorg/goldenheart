Veterans

Serving those who served.

Golden Heart works with veterans who may benefit from an individualized service-dog partnership and collaborates with veteran organizations as part of a broader community mission.

Veteran service-dog support

A dog trained around the veteran—not a one-size-fits-all program.

Veterans may have very different needs, routines, environments, and goals. Golden Heart approaches matching and training as an individual process, with the partnership—not simply the dog—at the center.

  • PTSD and psychiatric service-dog support
  • Mobility and other qualifying disability-related assistance
  • Matching based on compatibility and daily life
  • Handler preparation and transition support
  • Continued support after placement
