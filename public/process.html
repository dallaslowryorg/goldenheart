The Golden Heart Process

From first conversation to forever care.

Golden Heart does not use a one-size-fits-all formal application. The process begins with conversation, evaluation, and careful information gathering.

Get Started

Reach out and explain what you are looking for.

Consultation

Golden Heart begins learning about your needs and goals.

Video Meetings

Two Zoom or FaceTime meetings help Golden Heart learn more about the prospective client, daily life, and goals for the partnership.

Evaluation

Home, routine, references, and disability-related needs are reviewed.

Dog Selection

A potential match is considered based on compatibility and abilities.

Individual Training

The dog's work is shaped around the future handler.

Client Preparation

The handler learns how to work with and care for the dog.

Placement

The team moves into real-life routines together.

Graduation

A milestone recognizing the team's formal preparation.

Forever Care

Questions, refreshers, troubleshooting, and support continue.

Information Golden Heart may request

  • Two Zoom / FaceTime meetings
  • Pictures of the inside and outside of the home
  • Veterinarian and grooming reference or referral
  • An example of the applicant's day-to-day routine
  • Last two months of bank statements
  • Recommendation from a mental-health or medical professional

Protecting sensitive information

Do not send bank statements, medical records, or other sensitive documents through the general website inquiry form. The first step is simply to start the conversation.

If additional documentation is needed, Golden Heart can explain the appropriate way to provide it later in the process.

Forever Care

Graduation is a milestone—not goodbye.

Continued support can include follow-up training, troubleshooting, questions and guidance, team support, refresher training, transition assistance, continued communication, and community resources.
