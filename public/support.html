Partner With Golden Heart

Help strengthen service-dog partnerships and community connections.

Golden Heart's website vision includes relationships with businesses, veteran organizations, breeders, veterinarians, trainers, nonprofits, sponsors, and other community partners.

Sponsor a Team or Dog

Support can be directed toward training, veterinary care, food, supplies, travel, or other program needs once Golden Heart confirms its sponsorship structure.

Community Partnerships

Veteran organizations, businesses, trainers, and community groups can collaborate on education, events, referrals, and outreach.

Professional Partnerships

Breeders, veterinarians, groomers, and other dog-care professionals can help strengthen the support network around Golden Heart dogs and teams.

Golden Heart is identified in the supplied materials as Golden Heart Service Dogs, LLC. This site intentionally avoids describing payments or sponsorships as tax-deductible charitable donations unless Golden Heart confirms that treatment in writing.
