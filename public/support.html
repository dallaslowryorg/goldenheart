Partner With Golden Heart

Strong partnerships help strong teams succeed.

Golden Heart welcomes relationships with veteran organizations, businesses, breeders, veterinarians, groomers, trainers, community groups, and others who share a commitment to service-dog teams.

Sponsor a Dog or Team

Sponsorship support can help with training, veterinary care, food, supplies, travel, and other needs connected to preparing and supporting a service-dog team.

Veteran & Community Partnerships

Veteran organizations, businesses, and community groups can collaborate on outreach, education, events, referrals, and opportunities that strengthen access to service-dog support.

Professional Partnerships

Breeders, veterinarians, groomers, trainers, and other dog-care professionals can help build a dependable network around Golden Heart dogs and teams.

A shared mission

Support that reaches beyond a single placement.

Golden Heart's work includes training the dog, preparing the handler, supporting the transition, and staying connected after placement. Community and professional partners can play an important role at every stage.
