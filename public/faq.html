Frequently Asked Questions

Start with the questions most people have.

Every prospective team is different, but these answers explain the basic Golden Heart process and what to expect.

How do I get started with a service dog?

Golden Heart does not use a one-size-fits-all application. The process begins with an initial conversation, followed by consultation, two Zoom or FaceTime meetings, and individualized information gathering.

What disabilities do you work with?

Golden Heart works with qualifying needs that may include PTSD, psychiatric disabilities, autism support, medical alert needs, mobility assistance, and other disability-related needs evaluated individually.

How much does a service dog cost?

Current program ranges are Level One: $7,500–$12,500; Level Two: $13,000–$16,500; and Level Three: $18,000–$22,000. Final recommendations depend on the scope and complexity of the individual training plan.

How does the matching process work?

A dog is not matched by diagnosis alone. Golden Heart considers compatibility, lifestyle, daily routine, disability-related needs, home environment, the dog's temperament, and the dog's abilities.

Do you work with out-of-state clients?

Yes. Current travel fees are $3,500 within 500 miles and $4,200 within 900 miles. These fees help cover travel, lodging, airfare when applicable, and training-related travel during the three-to-six-month working period. For locations beyond 900 miles, contact Golden Heart to discuss options.

How long does training take?

Training timelines vary because each team and task plan is different. Out-of-state work may take place over a three-to-six-month timeframe, but there is no single universal timeline for every service-dog team.

What documentation may be requested?

Golden Heart may request two video meetings, photos of the inside and outside of the home, veterinary and grooming references or referrals, an example of the applicant's day-to-day routine, recent bank statements, and a recommendation from a medical or mental-health professional. Sensitive documents should not be sent through the general website inquiry form.

What happens after placement?

Golden Heart emphasizes continued support after placement, including follow-up training, troubleshooting, guidance, refresher training, transition assistance, continued communication, and community resources.

Do you offer continued support?

Yes. Forever Care is a core part of the Golden Heart approach. Graduation is a milestone, not the end of the relationship.
