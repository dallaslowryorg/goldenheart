About Golden Heart

Professional. Personal. Compassionate. Purpose-driven.

Golden Heart Service Dogs, LLC is built around the belief that a service dog is more than a trained animal—it is a working partnership that deserves thoughtful preparation and continued support.

Founder & Head Trainer

Nicole Ingraham

Nicole Ingraham is the Founder and Head Trainer of Golden Heart Service Dogs. Her work centers on thoughtful matching, individualized training, handler preparation, and the continued support that helps a service-dog team succeed beyond placement.

Golden Heart was built around a simple belief: the dog matters, the person matters, and the relationship between them deserves careful preparation from the first conversation through graduation and beyond.

What Golden Heart stands for

Care for the person. Care for the dog. Care about what comes next.

Thoughtful Matching

Compatibility, lifestyle, needs, temperament, and abilities all matter.

Individual Training

Every client/dog team is different, so training plans should reflect that reality.

Lifelong Support

The relationship continues through placement, transition, graduation, and beyond.
