About Golden Heart

Professional. Personal. Compassionate. Purpose-driven.

Golden Heart Service Dogs, LLC is a Tulsa-area service-dog business built around thoughtful matching, individualized training, handler education, and continued support for the working partnership.

Founder & Head Trainer

Nicole Ingraham

Nicole Ingraham is the Founder and Head Trainer of Golden Heart Service Dogs, LLC. Her passion for dogs began at a young age and grew into a career dedicated to helping dogs and the people who depend on them.

With more than a decade of hands-on experience, Nicole has worked with a wide variety of breeds, temperaments, behavioral challenges, obedience levels, and working-dog needs.

Her broader hands-on background includes puppy development and foundation work, basic and advanced obedience, off-leash obedience, behavior modification and rehabilitation, public-access preparation, therapy dogs, service dogs, psychiatric and PTSD-related service work, autism support, mobility assistance, medical alert/response work, personal-protection and working-dog training, and multi-dog household integration.

Nicole's professional development has also included learning and training under experienced professional dog trainers over the years. Rather than presenting Golden Heart around a list of formal certifications, the website emphasizes her decade-plus of practical, hands-on experience working with dogs and service-dog teams.

Golden Heart's current public services are focused on service-dog programs.

Over time, Nicole's work became increasingly focused on service dogs and the difference a highly trained working dog can make in a person's independence and quality of life. Golden Heart was created with that purpose at its center.

Training philosophy

Communication, consistency, trust, and individual understanding.

Every dog learns differently, and every client has different needs. Golden Heart takes an individualized approach rather than putting every dog and handler through exactly the same program.

Golden Heart specializes in service dogs for individuals with disabilities and specific medical or psychiatric needs, including PTSD, autism, mobility-related disabilities, psychiatric disabilities, and medical alert or response needs.

The philosophy is simple: train the dog, educate the handler, and support the team for the long term.

Based in Tulsa. Serving beyond Oklahoma.

Golden Heart is based in the Tulsa area and works with clients throughout Oklahoma and across the United States. Long-distance and international placements may also be considered individually when the training and transition plan is appropriate.
