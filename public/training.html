Training philosophy

Build the dog. Educate the person. Support the relationship.

Golden Heart emphasizes both sides of the partnership. Skills matter, but successful teams also need communication, realistic expectations, consistency, handler preparation, and continued support.

Training plans are individualized. Contact Golden Heart to discuss current scheduling, program length, and pricing for puppy, obedience, or therapy-dog training.
