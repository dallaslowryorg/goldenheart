Not General Pet Training

A specialized focus

Golden Heart does not currently offer general puppy training, basic pet obedience, advanced pet obedience, or off-leash pet-training programs as standalone services.

Training philosophy

Train the dog. Educate the handler. Support the team.

Golden Heart emphasizes both sides of the partnership. Skills matter, but successful working teams also need communication, realistic expectations, consistency, handler preparation, and continued support.

Training plans are individualized because no two service-dog teams have exactly the same needs, environment, or goals.
