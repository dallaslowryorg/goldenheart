Mara P. & Alfredo

OklahomaPTSDPsychiatric Service Dog9 Months of Team Training
“Then I met Alfredo, and my life changed.”
Read full testimonial

2025 was the most difficult year of my life. I faced challenges and battles that took me to the darkest place I have ever been. I desperately needed hope, light, and support.

Then I met Alfredo, and my life changed.

Through Golden Heart Service Dogs, Alfredo and I began a journey that would become life-changing for both of us. Over nine months of training, we worked together through countless experiences. From everyday errands and navigating busy stores to taking a flight I had been dreading and managing crowded environments. With each challenge, our bond grew stronger.

Today, Alfredo is more than my service dog—he is my constant companion and my lifeline. He often recognizes when I am struggling before I do, and he is there for me without hesitation or judgment. His unwavering support has helped me regain confidence, independence, and a sense of peace.

I am also incredibly grateful for Nicole. Her knowledge and training expertise were invaluable, but what meant even more was her genuine support throughout our journey. She was always there to answer questions, offer guidance, and encourage us every step of the way. It was clear that she truly cared about both Alfredo and me and wanted us to succeed.

There are not enough words to express how thankful I am for Nicole, Golden Heart Service Dogs, and, of course, Alfredo. They have made a profound difference in my life, and I will be forever grateful.

Mara P. • June 8, 2026 • Oklahoma

Scott & Ruby

OklahomaVeteranPTSDPsychiatric Support
“There are times when I don’t even have to say a word—she just knows.”
Read full testimonial

Golden Heart Service Dogs has had a lasting impact on my life through the incredible work they put into training Ruby.

From the very beginning, it was clear that Ruby had been given an amazing foundation. Her training goes beyond commands—it shows in her confidence, her calm presence, and the way she instinctively knows how to respond in the moments that matter most.

There are times when I don’t even have to say a word; she just knows. That kind of awareness and connection doesn’t happen by accident. It comes from the dedication, time, and heart that Golden Heart Service Dogs pours into every dog they train.

I especially want to recognize Nicole, the founder and trainer, whose work with Ruby truly shines through every single day. The care, attention, and passion she puts into each dog is evident, and it made all the difference. Her staff is just as incredible—professional, supportive, and clearly committed to the success of every dog they work with.

Ruby isn’t just well-trained; she’s steady, dependable, and always there when I need her. Because of that, I have a sense of comfort and confidence that I didn’t have before.

I will always be grateful for Golden Heart Service Dogs, Nicole, and her amazing team for shaping Ruby into the partner she is today.

Scott • Oklahoma veteran

Jerry & Ruger

VeteranPTSDPsychiatric Support
“This program gave me more than a service dog—it gave me a partner, a support system, and a connection to others who understand what I’ve been through.”
Read full testimonial

The support, guidance, and encouragement I’ve received from Golden Heart’s program have truly changed my life—and Ruger’s. They’ve made sure we’ve never had to face this journey alone.

What surprised me most was how much the program itself impacted me, not just receiving Ruger. Being part of the training, working side by side with other veterans, and going through the process together created a bond I didn’t expect—but one that felt familiar. It brought back a sense of camaraderie I hadn’t felt since my time in the military.

What they’ve built isn’t just a program—it’s a team. A team as strong and dependable as any I’ve been part of, both in and out of combat. Just like the bond between me and Ruger, they show up every day ready to support us and make sure we succeed.

They’ve handled every challenge that comes with training both veterans and service dogs with patience and determination. Whether it’s working through personal struggles or taking multiple dogs into a high-stress environment like a gun range, they meet every hurdle head-on and make it work.

This program gave me more than a service dog—it gave me a partner, a support system, and a connection to others who understand what I’ve been through. It reminded me that I’m not fighting alone.

Jerry • Veteran
