Understanding the journey

What each status means.

A dog's status reflects where that dog currently is in Golden Heart's training, matching, placement, or graduation process.

Available

Available / Prospect

The dog may be available for contract or being evaluated as a future service-dog prospect. Final matching depends on fit, needs, temperament, and training progress.

Matched

Matched / Transitioning

The dog has been paired with a client or is moving through transition and full-time placement. The dog is no longer available.

Partner Placement

Veteran Organization Placement

The dog has been transferred or placed with a veteran organization and is continuing its path through that partner program. This is separate from a Golden Heart dog paired directly with an individual veteran client.

Graduate

Golden Heart Graduate

The team has completed Golden Heart's formal training and transition process and has reached the graduation milestone.
