What happens next?

  1. Golden Heart reviews the initial inquiry or speaks with you by phone/email.
  2. The team can schedule a conversation to learn more about the prospective client.
  3. If appropriate, the process moves into two Zoom or FaceTime meetings and a more detailed pairing evaluation.
  4. Golden Heart may then request home photos, daily-routine details, veterinarian and groomer information, financial documentation, and a recommendation from an appropriate medical or mental-health provider.
  5. Sensitive documentation should be exchanged only through the secure process Golden Heart provides after initial contact.
