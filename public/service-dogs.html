Service Dog Programs

Individual training for individual lives.

Golden Heart's service-dog program is built around compatibility, disability-related needs, lifestyle, temperament, and the skills a particular dog can reliably perform.

Three program levels

Training scope grows with the complexity of the work.

Program ranges vary with the complexity of the training plan. Final recommendations depend on the individual team’s needs, goals, and required task work.

How matching works

A dog is not selected by diagnosis alone. Golden Heart considers the prospective handler's lifestyle, daily routine, disability-related needs, home environment, compatibility, and the dog's temperament and abilities.

Eligibility and specific assistance tasks are evaluated individually. A listed service type does not guarantee that every dog or applicant will be an appropriate match.

Out-of-state clients

Travel support is part of the program.

For out-of-state clients, additional travel fees help cover travel, training-related travel, lodging, and airfare when applicable during the three-to-six-month working timeframe.

  • Within 500 miles: $3,500 travel fee
  • Within 900 miles: $4,200 travel fee

For locations beyond 900 miles, contact Golden Heart to discuss availability, logistics, and travel costs.

What comes with the partnership

Training is only one part of the work.

Client Preparation

Transition Support

The team receives support as training moves into everyday routines and environments.

Continued Support
