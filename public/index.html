A different kind of service-dog program

The partnership comes first.

Golden Heart Service Dogs specializes in carefully trained service dogs and individualized training for veterans, children, adults, families, and people with qualifying disabilities. The goal is not simply to place a dog—it is to build a working partnership that can improve daily life.

The dog is trained for the person. The person is prepared for the dog. And the relationship continues after graduation.

Forever Care

The placement isn't the finish line.

Golden Heart believes service-dog teams deserve support after the dog goes home. Follow-up training, troubleshooting, refresher work, transition assistance, guidance, communication, and community resources are all part of the long-term picture.

When you receive a Golden Heart service dog, you become part of the Golden Heart family.
